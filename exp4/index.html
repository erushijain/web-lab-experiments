Our Story

The Good Habit began with a simple idea: sustainable living should be easy, not overwhelming. Founded in 2020, we set out to replace everyday single-use items with products that are made to last.

Every item is ethically sourced, plastic-free and designed for daily use. We work with small makers who care about fair wages and low-impact materials.

Because small habits, repeated daily, add up to a big difference for the planet.
